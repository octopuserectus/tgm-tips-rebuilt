Tetris: The Grand Master series guide

This guide covers three games by Arika:

  • Tetris: The Grand Master (TGM)
  • Tetris: The Absolute – The Grand Master 2 PLUS (TAP): Master and Death modes
  • Tetris: The Grand Master 3 – Terror-Instinct (Ti): Master and Shirase modes, Classic rule only
{% include chapter-index.html %}

Terminology

English terms follow tetris.wiki. The glossary lists the Japanese terms of the original and the English used for each.

The original names pieces by their TGM colors; here they are called by letter: I red, T cyan, S purple, Z green, J blue, L orange, O yellow.
